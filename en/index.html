/ About

twenty years of images

Art director with over 20 years in visual creation: web design, graphic design, illustration.

Nuit Américaine is the film studio I founded in 2026, after twenty years in art direction. The name is the French term for day-for-night, the cinema technique that shoots night scenes in broad daylight: the art of making a fabricated image believable.

I work as on a shoot, without the shoot. Art direction, storyboard, framing, light, editing, grading: every image is decided before it is generated, then finished by hand. No set, no crew, deadlines in days.

Based in La Rochelle, I work in Paris and remotely, in French and in English, for brands, agencies and public bodies.
