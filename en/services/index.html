/ Services

four ways
to work together

Four ways to work together, depending on whether you need to test an idea, deliver a film, hold a world over time or feed your channels. For each: what I deliver, and how long it takes.

01Previsualisation

Test an idea before committing to production. I turn the brief into art direction and a storyboard, then into AI images and sequences that show the film: framing, light, rhythm, tone. The result is made for deciding: settling a concept, convincing internally, scoping a production.

/typical timingA few days to a week

/deliverables

  • Documented art direction (references, palette, light)
  • Storyboard of the key shots
  • AI previsualisation sequences
  • Boards of key stills
  • Creative rounds included

02Brand film

A short film delivered ready to publish, produced entirely with AI imagery and video. From writing the creative direction to editing and finishing, with the same standards as a conventional production.

/typical timingTwo to three weeks

/deliverables

  • A choice of creative directions
  • Edited and graded film
  • Format adaptations
  • Source files delivered

03Art direction

Long-term support for a team or a brand: building a visual world, keeping produced content consistent, setting up an AI production workflow that stays under control.

/typical timingAt your team's pace, monthly

/deliverables

  • Visual world and image rules
  • Documented production workflow
  • Creative follow-up and decisions
  • Method handed over to the team

04Social media content

Series of images and short videos spun from one world, in vertical format, ready to post. The brand film becomes ten pieces of content, without ten productions.

/typical timingFirst series in one to two weeks

/deliverables

  • Reels and vertical formats
  • Visual series, by season or by product
  • A graphic world that holds from post to post
  • Per project or monthly

/ Method

every image
is a decision

Five steps, in this order, on every project. The time goes upstream, deciding, and downstream, finishing the image. The generation itself is the shortest part.

  1. Days 1 to 3

    01.Art direction

    Vague references→A fixed course

    Building a coherent visual world: references, palette, light, framing. Nothing is generated before the direction is settled.

  2. Days 3 to 5

    02.Storyboard

    A brief→A drawn film

    The brief becomes a sequence of shots: framing, values, transitions. Every shot is decided on paper before the first image is generated.

  3. Week 2

    03.AI previsualisation

    An abstract brief→A tested film

    Cinematic previsualisation of the campaign with AI images and video, ahead of production. The film is approved before production is committed.

  4. Weeks 2 to 3

    04.Storytelling & editing

    Isolated shots→A story that holds

    Motion design and visual storytelling: rhythm, transitions, editing. What makes a film hold beyond the beautiful image.

  5. Final days

    05.Image finishing

    Raw render→A believable image

    Colour, framing, composition. Taking the raw render to an image that stands next to real footage.
