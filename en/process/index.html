/ Process

one shot,
from idea to screen

Seven steps, in this order, and at each one a real piece from the project: episode 3 of the mini-series, Mamekan in Asakusa, 33 shots, 1 min 20. We follow shot 2, the business visit, from the breakdown to the edit.

  1. 01

    The script

    Everything starts with a written breakdown: every shot with its number, its length and what it shows. Thirty-three lines for this episode. It is the reference for every step that follows, and the document a client approves before a single image exists.

    014 sAsakusa temple, wide shot
    024 sBusiness visit: the crystal glasses, the black briefcase
    033 sShe points to the window, knowingly

  2. 02

    The sheets

    Before any scene is generated, every character and every set has a sheet: a face fixed once and for all, a place seen from several angles. That is what keeps Kenji looking like Kenji from shot to shot, and keeps the sweet shop from changing its counter.

  3. 03

    The storyboard

    The breakdown becomes drawn panels: framing, shot size, gesture. Decisions are made on paper before the first image is generated. Here, the first board, six panels out of thirty-three.

  4. 04

    The key frames

    One image per shot, in colour and in the final style. This is where the iterating happens, not later: fixing an image costs fourteen times less than fixing a video. Shot 3: in the first version the gesture goes to the right. In the version kept, it goes towards the window, and the space matches the previous shot.

  5. 05

    The animatic

    The key frames laid end to end, at the lengths of the breakdown, with the music. We watch the film before it exists: the rhythm is approved here, and video is only launched once the animatic is signed off.

  6. 06

    The video

    Each key frame becomes the starting point of its shot. Shot 2, two takes: in the first, she raises her cup. In the second, she points to the window, the gesture that leads into the next shot. That is the one that stays.

  7. 07

    Editing and sound

    The shots are cut to the planned lengths, with a light grain to unify the image. The sound is built separately: for this episode, three music cues, seven ambiences and twenty-one sound effects, then a level normalised for social platforms. Shot 2, as it is in the film.
